## Promotion thresholds

Mixed. Paper 1 code and short Paper 2 questions are points-marked: developing ≈ 70% supported,
secure ≈ 80% unaided. Paper 2 level-of-response questions are descriptor-marked: developing =
band below target supported; secure = target band unaided, twice, on different tasks. Say which
bar was applied in every evidence string.

## Behavioural metrics

1. **Blanks per paper**, especially trace tables and the extended-response questions — the
   known failure mode carried from maths and English.
2. **Handwritten programs per fortnight** — Paper 1 is handwritten Python; at least one program
   on paper, photographed and marked, every two weeks. Record it in the session evidence.
