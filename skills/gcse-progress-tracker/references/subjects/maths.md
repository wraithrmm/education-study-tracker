## Promotion thresholds

Points-marked throughout, so the default bars apply: developing ≈ 70% with support, secure ≈ 80%
unaided (4/4, 5/6), examready on a spaced re-test ≥3 weeks later.

## Behavioural metric

**Blanks per paper**, which should be falling toward zero. The known failure mode is skipping
questions rather than not knowing them — back-of-paper blanks are a pace and confidence problem
as often as a knowledge one, and the two need separating in any report.

## Checkpoint duties — mandatory

- **Dec 2026 Foundation full mock:** expected ≥ grade 5 (June-2022 boundary 172/240 equivalent).
  Below that → recommend extending Phase 2 before starting the Higher layer. Log all three papers
  as one attempt.
- **Jan 2027 pre-mock + Feb 2027 Higher mock — the tier decision.** Trending below ~55/240 on
  Higher → flag clearly to the parent that Foundation entry should be discussed *before* entries
  close (~21 Feb 2027). ≥ ~90/240 → grade 5 on track. **This flag is mandatory whichever way it
  points**, and is raised in plain words, early enough to act on.
- Remind the parent of entry-logistics deadlines when a checkpoint report lands within 6 weeks of
  one.

## Foundation before Higher

Never treat a Higher-only topic as a priority while its Foundation prerequisite sits at `gap`.
Any 🔴 Foundation topic outranks any 🟠 Higher topic. The review queue already orders by this;
don't override it without saying why in the session summary.

## Grade conversion

Boundaries are stored on the subject row and applied by the service. The full published tables,
the notional single-paper cross-check and the worked method live in
`gcse-maths-marker/references/gcse-grading-standards.md`. A single 80-mark paper is scaled ×3 to
/240 before conversion and reported with the "one paper of three is a noisy estimate" caveat.
