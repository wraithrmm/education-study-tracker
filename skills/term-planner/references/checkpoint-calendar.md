## Lead-in rules

- Any row within **6 weeks** is quoted with its lead-in actions every time the calendar is
  answered or a checkpoint report is written.
- Mocks are logged as **one attempt per sitting**, all papers, with `sat_on` per paper, by the
  subject's marker skill. A mock that is not logged has not happened.
- A phase shift is proposed by the planner after the checkpoint that precedes it, and written
  only on the parent's word.
