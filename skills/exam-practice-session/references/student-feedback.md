# What may be in `student_feedback`

She reads this on the portal, beside the question and what she wrote, for as long as the record
exists. It is the only part of the marking she sees.

## The rule

**The topic and a method hint. Never the answer.** She should be able to re-work the question
from the feedback and find the answer herself. If the feedback makes re-working pointless, it
has gone too far.

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| "Divide the top by the bottom." | "= 0.4375" |
| "Take out every common factor, including the letter." | "2x(3x + 4)" |
| "Write the place values 8 4 2 1 above the digits and add." | "11" |
| "Name the technique and say what it makes the reader feel." | A model sentence she could copy |
| "This needed a second quotation with its own comment." | The quotation |
| "A trace table needs a row per iteration." | The completed table |

## The shape

One or two sentences. Where there is something to credit, credit it first — it is true and it
makes the rest readable:

> Method fully right. The answer line wanted a number, not a length — check what the question
> asked for.

> Good: the effect on the reader is explained. Add a second technique with its own quotation to
> reach the top of Level 2.

> Left blank with five minutes unused. Binary to denary: write the place values above the digits
> and add them up.

For a full-mark answer, say what earned it, briefly. "Full marks — working shown clearly" tells
her which habit to keep.

## Never

- Never the answer, the number, the quotation or the model sentence.
- Never the mark scheme's wording, or the phrase "the mark scheme says".
- Never a comparison to anyone else, or to a grade.
- Never "you should have known this". The paper tests technique.
- Never more than two sentences. The technique target comes once, in the chat, for the whole
  paper — not per question.

## The marker's note is different

`note` on the same call is the teacher-side record: the scheme codes, the band matched, exactly
why the marks went the way they did. It renders only behind Dad's login and it should be as
precise as a real marker's — that is what makes the record worth keeping.
