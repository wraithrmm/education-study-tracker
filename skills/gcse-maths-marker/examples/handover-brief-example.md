## 5. UK curriculum & exam context (plan to the right target)

- **Grading:** GCSEs in England are graded **9 (high) to 1 (low)**. Foundation tier awards **grades 1–5** (5 is the ceiling); Higher tier awards **4–9**.
- **Structure:** AQA 8300 is **three papers × 80 marks = 240 total** (Paper 1 non-calculator; Papers 2 & 3 calculator), 100% exam. The grade is set on the **240 total** using **grade boundaries set fresh each series** (Ofqual "comparable outcomes"), not a fixed percentage.
- **This student's grade in context:** 49/80 scales to ~147/240; June 2022 Foundation grade-4 boundary was 135. On **2024–2026 standards** the grade-4 boundary is higher (~157–160), so the same performance is a **borderline 3/4** today.
- **2026 standards:** comparable to summer 2025 (pre-pandemic standard, restored 2023). A **formula sheet is provided** in exams for 2025–2027 — so the plan should prioritise *selecting and using* formulae (e.g. area, Pythagoras) over rote memorisation.
- **Content areas (AQA/DfE):** Number; Algebra; Ratio, proportion & rates of change; Geometry & measures; Probability; Statistics. Map each §4 topic to its exact spec reference in your research.
